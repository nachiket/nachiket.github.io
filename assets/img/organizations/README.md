# Organization mark sources

These marks identify organizations named next to them in the CV. They do not imply endorsement.

- `waterloo.svg`: University of Waterloo official profile icon, from `uwaterloo.ca`.
- `caltech.png`: Caltech public identity-site wordmark, from `identity.caltech.edu`.
- `penn.svg`: shield cropped from the University of Pennsylvania's official homepage SVG lockup.
- `imperial.png`: current 2024 Imperial wordmark, sourced from the official artwork mirrored by Wikimedia Commons and resized for the web.
- `nserc.png`: official NSERC/CRSNG Federal Identity Program digital-colour kit.
- `mitacs.svg`: official Mitacs website wordmark.
- `huawei.png`: official Huawei corporate-site mark.
- `tenstorrent.png`: Tenstorrent's official website icon. Tenstorrent requests review of stylized-mark usage through `logoreview@tenstorrent.com`.

The AMD Arrow is derived from AMD's corporate mark; the Xilinx mark is from Simple Icons; and the Lattice marks are cropped from Lattice Semiconductor's official colour and reverse logo files. Those shared affiliation assets live in `assets/img/affiliations/`.
